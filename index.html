About

Developing Practical Expertise Across Technology, Finance and Beyond

I am Laura Efe Badua Abban, a student with a strong interest in technology, computing, finance, and practical digital skills. I focus on developing useful skills through continuous learning, practical experience, and sharing knowledge with others.

My work includes providing practical computing tutoring, creating educational ebooks, and offering personal finance education through individual sessions. I also have a personal interest in investing and day trading, areas in which I continue to develop my knowledge and experience.

I value continuous learning, clear communication, and practical application. My goal is to build meaningful work at the intersection of technology, finance, education, and digital skills.

Personal Finance Education

I provide practical personal finance education through individual sessions designed to help learners develop stronger money-management habits and a clearer understanding of financial concepts.
